```
奥普塔罗工作室的官方 App：从你此刻真实的困惑出发，亲手切牌、抽牌，再获得逐张牌位的解读。想深入聊聊时，可以直接预约工作室的真人塔罗师。

78 张完整牌面，正逆位，切牌后逐张亲手选牌；牌序保存在服务器上，重新打开结果不变。

【逐张牌位的解读】
每张牌结合它所在的位置和你的问题来解读，最后给出可以落地的行动建议。注册后还可以追问、补一张澄清牌。

【真人塔罗师】
需要更深入的讨论时，选择文字报告或语音咨询，填好期望时间，工作室确认后进行。AI 解读也可以一键转给真人继续看。

【真实的算牌案例】
经提问者授权、工作室审核后匿名公开的真实案例：问题、牌阵、每张牌和完整解读，可以点赞、评价和分享。

【塔罗学习】
78 张牌的牌义图鉴：大阿卡纳与四组小阿卡纳，每张牌的正位、逆位与读牌方法。

【每日一牌提醒】
在你选择的时间提醒你抽一张今日牌，由手机本地发出，无需登录。

我们把塔罗当作帮助思考的工具：牌面提供观察角度，不预言、不保证任何结果，也不替代医疗、法律或投资等专业意见。

## Description (English)

```
The official app of Ops Tarot Studio. Start from the question that's really on your mind, cut and draw your own cards, and get a position-by-position reading — or book one of the studio's tarot readers when you want a real conversation.

QUESTION FIRST, THEN THE SPREAD
Write what you'd like clarity on and the app suggests a spread that fits the shape of your question: single card, three cards, relationship, career, two or three options, the year ahead, the twelve houses, the Celtic Cross and more — 20 spreads, and you can always choose your own.

YOUR OWN HANDS ON THE DECK
All 78 cards with reversals. Cut the deck and pick each card yourself; the order is kept on our server, so your reading is the same whenever you come back.

A READING FOR EACH POSITION
Every card is read in its position and against your question, ending with practical next steps. With a free account you can ask follow-ups and draw a clarifier.

REAL READERS
Book a written report or a voice session with a studio reader at a time that suits you. You can hand an AI reading over to a reader in one tap.

REAL, ANONYMOUS EXAMPLES
Readings shared with the asker's permission and reviewed by the studio: the question, the spread, every card and the full reading.

LEARN THE CARDS
A 78-card library with upright and reversed meanings and how to read each card.

DAILY CARD REMINDER
A gentle local reminder at the time you choose. No account needed.

We treat tarot as a tool for reflection: the cards offer perspectives, not predictions or guaranteed outcomes, and never replace medical, legal or financial advice.
